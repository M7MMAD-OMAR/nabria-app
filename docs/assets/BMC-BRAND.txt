bmc-button.svg and bmc-logo.svg are Buy Me a Coffee's own brand assets, taken
unmodified from the brand kit they publish at buymeacoffee.com. They are not
ours to recolour, restyle or redraw, which is why they sit here as files rather
than being folded into icons.svg with the rest of the page's marks.

The yellow in them is #FFDD00 and the ink is #0D0C22. Both appear in site.css
only inside the support section's own rules, never in the palette at the top of
the file: that block is the application's own colours and nothing else belongs
in it.
