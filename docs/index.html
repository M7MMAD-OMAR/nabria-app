02 / Local by design

It stays
here.

Your audio, model, transcript and history live on your machine. Nabria works with the network off, because dictation should not need a destination.

Five states.

04 / Real, not staged

Small setup.
Then disappear.

The first launch asks only what it needs: a model, a microphone and a shortcut. After that, Nabria becomes a quiet part of the way you write.
